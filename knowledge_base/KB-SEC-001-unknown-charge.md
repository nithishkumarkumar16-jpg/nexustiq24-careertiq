title: Unknown charges and account security
category: security
applies_to: [broadband, mobile]
keywords: [unknown charge, fraud, unrecognized, scam, charged]
---
# Scope
Use whenever a customer reports an unrecognized charge or possible account compromise.
# Verification checks
Do not ask for card numbers or security credentials. Review only the available account charge summary.
# Approved resolution
Do not validate or deny a suspected fraudulent charge based only on chat text.
# Escalation criteria
Immediately transfer the case to the account-security team.
