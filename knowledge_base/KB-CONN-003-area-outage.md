# Scope
Use only when the account service status identifies a known area outage.
# Verification checks
Confirm the customer's service status is outage-affected.
# Approved resolution
Tell the customer that their area is affected by a known network outage and that network operations is working on restoration. Do not provide an estimated restoration time unless it is in the account record.
# Escalation criteria
Escalate if the account is not marked outage-affected or the customer reports an emergency or safety concern.
