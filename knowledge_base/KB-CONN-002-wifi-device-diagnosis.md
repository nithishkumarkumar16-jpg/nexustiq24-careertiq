title: Wi-Fi issue on one device
category: connectivity
applies_to: [broadband]

---
# Scope
Use when only one device has a Wi-Fi problem while the broadband service remains active.
# Verification checks
Confirm that other devices can connect before treating the case as a single-device Wi-Fi issue.
# Approved resolution
Ask the customer to forget and reconnect to the Wi-Fi network on the affected device.
# Escalation criteria
Escalate if the customer cannot identify whether other devices are affected after a targeted follow-up.
