category: billing
applies_to: [broadband, mobile]

# Scope
Use for known account payment status.
# Verification checks
Check whether a payment is recorded and whether the account is overdue.
# Approved resolution
State the recorded payment status. Do not confirm a payment that is not recorded.
# Escalation criteria
Escalate when the customer says a payment was made but the account record does not show it.
