# Scope
Use when the account's recent charge summary explains a higher invoice.
# Verification checks
Review the latest invoice amount and the account record's recent charge summary before explaining a charge.
# Approved resolution
Explain only the charge listed in the account record. If the charge is not represented in the account record, do not speculate.
# Escalation criteria
Escalate an unrecognized charge, a suspected fraudulent charge, or a charge absent from the account record.
